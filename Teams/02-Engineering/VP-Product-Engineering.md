# VP of Product Engineering

## Position Details
- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports To:** CTO
- **Direct Reports:** Lead Frontend Engineer, Senior Frontend Engineer(s), Lead Backend Engineer, Senior Backend Engineer(s), Senior Full-Stack Engineer(s)
- **Employment Type:** Full-time

## Role Summary
Leads the engineering team dedicated to Number Pii's own products. Ensures product development is ring-fenced from client work. Owns the technical roadmap, sprint execution, and code quality for all product codebases.

## Core Skills
- Engineering leadership and mentorship
- Sprint planning and Agile execution
- Technical architecture and system design (@software-architecture)
- Code review and quality standards (@code-review-excellence)
- Cross-functional collaboration with Product & Design (@internal-comms)
- Performance optimisation and scalability (@web-performance-optimization)
- Technical debt management and prioritisation (@architecture-decision-records)

## Technical Skills
- Next.js, React, TypeScript (@react-best-practices)
- Node.js, PostgreSQL, GraphQL (@postgresql)
- AWS (EC2, Lambda, RDS, S3) (@aws-serverless)
- CI/CD pipelines, Docker
- Testing strategies (unit, integration, e2e)
- Monitoring and observability

## Project Involvement
