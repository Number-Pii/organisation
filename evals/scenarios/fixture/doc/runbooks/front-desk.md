# Front Desk Runbook

Written by the operations team in 2025 — before the booking service existed —
and kept as the reference for desk staff.

## Taking a booking
1. Check the room calendar — every room has its own.
2. Enter the guest's name exactly as on their ID.
3. Quote the price in pounds; the system stores pence.

## Cancellations
Refunds follow the policy in `doc/specs/payments.md` — staff never promise
anything beyond it. Escalate disputes to the duty manager.

## Opening hours
The desk is staffed 08:00–18:00 on weekdays.
